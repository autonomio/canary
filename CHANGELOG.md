# Changelog

## v0.6.0 on 27th of November, 2026

- Add a study table. Readers can see which reports describe each study, how sure the match is and what each study measured, with every value linked to its passage and to the copy of the report it came from. Results follow the outcome definitions in the protocol, keep calculations reproducible and show missing results explicitly. Each edition of the table is kept, and claims that used a changed value are flagged.

## v0.5.0 on 27th of November, 2026

- Add dated review protocols. Canary saves the plan before it searches and records each amendment with its reason and timing. Readers can see what changed, who approved each version and whether the review was registered. The dated protocol downloads from the dashboard, CLI and API.

## v0.4.0 on 26th of November, 2026

- Add a methods appendix to the PRISMA dashboard. Readers can see which searches ran, why screening stopped and which passages support each claim. The appendix travels with the review archive.

## v0.3.0 on 26th of November, 2026

- Add a PRISMA checklist to finished reviews. Readers can find each recorded answer, see what is missing and trace author statements to the reviewer. The checklist is available in the dashboard, CLI and API and as a standalone HTML report.

## v0.2.0 on 26th of November, 2026

- Add a history of review decisions. Readers can see why a paper was included or excluded, who made the decision and what evidence they cited.
- Add access to earlier editions and corrections through the dashboard, CLI, HTTP API and MCP.
